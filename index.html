Simulating Aggregate Loss Distributions ↗

Operational risk through the Loss Distribution Approach: combining event frequency and severity with Monte Carlo simulation.

Methodology

Event frequency is modeled with a Poisson distribution and severity with a log-normal distribution. Iterative sampling combines both components to simulate aggregate operational losses.
